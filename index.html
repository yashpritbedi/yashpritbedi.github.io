About me

Hi, I'm Yashprit — the person who notices the packaging before the product, the ad before the show, and the story before the sale.

I'm an MBA student at BITSoM, but the more honest introduction is this: I'm someone who collects small observations about people — what they choose, why they choose it, and how the right words or the right shade of colour can change how they feel about themselves.

I bring energy into rooms, ask one question more than necessary, and genuinely enjoy turning messy ideas into things people can see and feel.

What beauty means to me

"To me, beauty isn't about looking perfect — it's the quiet confidence of feeling most like yourself. It's my grandmother's kajal, my best friend's fearless red lipstick, and the two minutes in front of the mirror that turn an ordinary morning into intention. Beauty is self-respect, made visible."

— Yashprit Bedi
One L'Oréal brand I relate to most

KÉRASTASE

"You Dare. We Care ." isn't just a tagline to me — it's the energy I grew up borrowing. My go-to brand from the L’Oréal portfolio is Kérastase. My long hair has always been a part of my identity, and taking care of it has always been important to me. I relate to Kérastase because it makes hair care feel more than just a routine—it combines science, expertise, and luxury to create a personalised experience. For me, my hair is something I have grown with and taken pride in, which is why Kérastase is a brand I genuinely connect with.
